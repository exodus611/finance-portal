🛡️ Cybershield: protection from financial scammers in Israel

A summary of current deception schemes according to the Israeli National Cyber ​​Security Authority (מערך הסייבר הלאומי). Examples of real SMS, calls and phishing sites.

Suspicious call or SMS?
Emergency hotline of the Israeli National Cyber ​​Headquarters (free, 24/7):
📞 119

Typical scam schemes and how to recognize them

📞 Call from the “Russian-speaking bank security service”

Simulate calls Leumi, Hapoalim, Discount, Mizrahi
CALL

The caller introduces himself as a security officer, reports a “suspicious transfer to Dubai or abroad” and demands to transfer money to a “secure account” or provide a one-time SMS code.

How to proceed: Real bank never does not ask to transfer money to other accounts and never asks for SMS codes over the phone. Hang up immediately and call your bank using the number on the back of your card (*5522, *2424, etc.).

📲 SMS from Israel Post (Doar Israel) with payment of duty

Fake payment link for 12–35 shekels
SMS

You receive a message about a delayed package with a link to a fake website that requires you to enter your credit card number and CVV code.

“Your parcel IL983421 has been detained at customs. Pay 14.80 ₪ for delivery: israel-post-pay.cc"
How to proceed: Check the status of parcels only on the official website israelpost.co.il. Israeli state domains end only with .gov.il or .co.il.

🏛️ “Tax refund” or “Payment from Bituach Leumi”

Phishing of the personal account of government agencies
GOSPORTAL

A message about an alleged refund of 1,200–4,500 ₪ with a request to “confirm your bank details” via a link.

How to proceed: Bituach Leumi and the Tax Service transfer the money directly to the account registered in your system. Login to your personal account is carried out only through the government portal gov.il.

Golden rules of financial security in Israel

  1. Do not dictate SMS codes to anyone. The text in the SMS with the code clearly states: “Do not share this code with anyone, not even a bank employee.”.
  2. Never install remote access programs (AnyDesk, TeamViewer) if you are asked to do so by phone.
  3. Check the domain name of the link. Websites of government agencies end strictly with .gov.il, banks - on .co.il. No domains .cc, .top, .site.
  4. Do you have any doubts? Call 119 to Cyber ​​Headquarters or 100 to the police.
