Wellness (דמי הבראה) - the rate was raised

On August 18, an expansion order was issued: a day of wellness in the private sector now costs 451.50 ₪ instead of 418 ₪. If you were already paid at the old rate, your employer must pay the difference.

What you need to know

Briefly about the rules

  • The right appears after a year of work. Full 12 months with one employer.
  • The order applies to almost everyone. The exception is the public sector, link to its tariffs and protected enterprises.
  • If you paid 418 ₪ you have to pay extra. The Ministry of Labor clarified: the period for which they are paid is considered, not the date of payment. It doesn’t matter whether they were paid in one sum, in parts or as part of a salary.
  • Partial rate is prorated. Half-rate is half the amount.
  • They usually pay in the summer from June to September.
  • Not included in the calculation of pensions and compensation. This is a separate payment, not part of the salary.
  • For unpaid leave (חל"ת) health benefits are not accrued.
  • Upon dismissal unpaid health benefits are included in the final payment.

Source: extension order of 08/18/2026 to the collective agreement of 06/22/2026 between the Histadrut and the Presidium of Business Organizations.
