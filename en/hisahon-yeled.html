Savings for each child (חיסכון לכל ילד)

The state deposits 58 ₪ per month into the account of each child under 18 years of age. Where this money is and how much it will grow depends on the choice of the parent. The difference between “did nothing” and “set up” is up to five times.

Where is my money and how to check it

Five minutes in your personal account

  1. Log in to your personal account Bituah leumi — btl.gov.il
  2. Look at three things: where there is money (bank or fund), what route selected and How many has already accumulated
  3. Official website of the program with the Bituach Leumi calculator - hly.gov.il
  4. You can compare the profitability and commissions of all funds on the state portal Gemel-no
What is important to know:
  • If the parent has not chosen anything, the money goes by default to a high risk fund - for a long term, this is usually more profitable than a bank, but many do not know about this.
  • You can transfer from a bank to a fund, but not back. From 2025, it is allowed to stop contributions to the bank and open a fund.
  • The route within the fund and the fund itself are changing at any time and for free.
  • Commissions under 21 are paid by Bituach Leumi, not you.
  • Money is written down in the child's name, you can pick them up at 18 years old; if you wait until 21, the state will add another bonus.
  • If the child has a life-threatening illness, the money is allowed to be withdrawn earlier, by decision of the medical board.
