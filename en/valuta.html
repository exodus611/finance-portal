What course do you need

There are two different courses in use in Israel, and confusion between them costs money.

Official rate of the Bank of Israel

"Shaar Yatzig" (שער יציג), shaar yatsig - representative course. Published weekdays around 9:20 a.m. and valid all day. It is used to calculate taxes, customs duties, court amounts and reporting. It is not updated on weekends and holidays - the rate of the last working day is valid.

Market rate

Changes constantly throughout the day. It is closer to what you will see during a transfer or in an exchanger. But it is not equal to what you receive in your hands: banks and exchangers add their own markup.

How much is lost in exchange? The bank usually takes 1.5-3% of the amount, the exchange office in a tourist place - up to 5%, and the card when paying abroad adds a conversion fee of about 2.5-3%. On a thousand dollars, the difference between a good and a bad exchange rate easily reaches 100–150 shekels. Compare with the rate on this page - it shows the base without any extra charges.

Frequently asked questions

Why is the rate here different from the rate at my bank?

Shown here is the basic rate without extra charges. Banks and exchangers add their own margin - usually 1.5-3%, plus sometimes a fixed commission for the transaction. By comparing your rate with the base rate, you will immediately see how much they charge you on top.

What course is needed for tax and customs?

The official exchange rate of the Bank of Israel on the desired date. It is also used when calculating duties on parcels and when declaring income from abroad.

Why doesn't the exchange rate change on weekends?

The Bank of Israel publishes the official rate only on weekdays. On Friday evenings, Saturdays and holidays the last working day rate applies. The market rate may move slightly.

Are the cart data sent somewhere?

No. Everything is stored in the memory of your browser and is not transferred anywhere. If you clear your browser data, the trash bin will disappear. You can share the link to the cart: the amounts are added directly to the page address.

How recent is the data?

Market rates are updated several times a day, the official Bank of Israel rate is updated on weekday mornings. The exact time of the last update is shown below the rates line. If there is no Internet, the page will show the last saved values ​​and warn about this.
