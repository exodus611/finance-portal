FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Are the calculators exact, or just an estimate?

These are model calculations using standard formulas (annuity, tax brackets, etc.) — they show the big picture and the right order of magnitude. Actual terms depend on your situation, the bank, the date and current law.

Do I need to sign up to use them?

No. All calculations happen right in your browser — nothing is sent to a server or stored anywhere.

Is this a bank’s or an advisor’s website?

No. This is an independent information project — we are affiliated with no bank, broker or advisor, and earn no commission on anything you sign.

What is “auto-pick” in the mashkanta calculator?

The algorithm sifts through 67,000+ combinations of track shares and terms to find the optimal mix for your goal — instead of you tuning proportions by hand.

Why are terms translated with Hebrew in brackets?

So you grasp the idea in your own language and still recognise the official term on Hebrew bank documents or payslips.
