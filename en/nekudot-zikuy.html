Base score and woman score

Every Israeli resident receives 2.25 points basic. Women receive extra +0.5 points (total 2.75) - this is a separate long-standing benefit not related to children.

Children are the most complex part of the system

Main rule: ONE parent receives points for children - the one who is registered as a recipient of child benefits (קצבת ילדים) from Bituach Leumi, by default the mother (can be transferred to the father by statement). Points are not doubled for both parents in a regular family. In case of divorce or separation, the custodial parent receives both shares, the second - only his own.

By age: year of birth - +1 point for each parent (separate allowance, does not depend on the recipient). Next for the benefit recipient: 1–5 years - according to various sources from 2.5 to 4.5 points, there are real discrepancies even between professional sites - check the exact figure in the simulator. 6–17 years old - the share of the mother and father is NOT equal: historically this benefit was only maternal, recently the father receives a smaller share.

Repatriate - two different schemes depending on the date

The law changed in 2022 (amendment No. 262), so the scheme depends on the date of repatriation:

Until January 1, 2022: the first 18 months - 4.5 points, the next year - another 2 points, the last year - another 1 point. Total 42 months.

From January 1, 2022: the first 12 months - 1 point, the next 18 months - another 4.5 points, then approximately another 2 and 1 point according to the old scheme. The total duration is 54 months. The calculator automatically selects the desired scheme based on your repatriation date.

Diploma

1 point for the first degree (bachelor's degree), another 0.5 for the second degree (master's degree) from a recognized university. Valid for no longer than 3 years from the start of use, you can start a year after release or a year later - your choice.

Demobilized

The right is valid for 36 months after demobilization. The exact size of the point depends on the type and duration of service - check with the personnel department or with an accountant; an average estimate is used here.
