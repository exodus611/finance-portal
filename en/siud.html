Siyud (סיעוד) - benefits for those who need care

Bituach Leumi pays for elderly care: from ₪1,705 per month (level 1) up to 7,440 ₪ (level 6) - or hours of home care. Payment is required declare, it is not assigned automatically. Check to see if your parents are eligible.

How can I get Level 1 - entirely in cash: 1,705 ₪ (1 hour of care = 310 ₪). From level 2 - mainly care hours, some hours can be exchanged for money. Example (level 3, from 04/01/2026): 13 hours of care per week + 992 ₪ in cash instead of 17 hours.
The income test does not cancel the payment immediately If the income is above the threshold, the payment is reduced to 50%, and not canceled immediately: 50% is valid up to 20,654 ₪ (single) and up to 30,980 ₪ (couple) of total income.

What counts as income The pension and old-age benefit “Ezrach Vatik” are taken into account (קצבת זיקנה, officially - קצבת אזרח ותיק) recipient. If both spouses claim the right, the joint income is divided in half, and the right for each is considered separately. For each child in the family, a supplement is added to the threshold (the amount is on the official website).
3

Who is entitled to this

All four conditions at once

  • Age. Men - from 67 years old. Women - from 60–65 years old, depending on the year of birth (as the retirement age).
  • Living at home. The person lives at home (living in an elderly care home is determined by separate rules).
  • Needs help. Dependency score of at least 2.5 points: difficulty with hygiene, clothing, nutrition, movement, urinary control - checked by an evaluator at home.
  • Income test (table above).
Repatriates - from day one The new ole in the state of siuda is entitled to payment from the 8th day after arrival - even without Israeli contribution experience. If your parents have recently arrived and you are caring for them, check your rights, many people don’t know.
4

How to submit - step by step

The most common mistake: “no one came because no one applied”

  1. Gather medical documents. Diagnoses, doctors’ reports (all those involved), a list of medications, information about rehabilitation equipment (stroller, walker, wheelchair). The fresher the better.
  2. Apply at Bituach Leumi. Online at btl.gov.il (section “applications”) or at the branch. If you are applying for a parent, a power of attorney (can be issued in Bituach Leumi (National Insurance), the form is available on site).
  3. In-home assessment. An institute employee comes and conducts an addiction test. The result determines level 1–6.
  4. Solution. Bituach Leumi sends a decision on the level and amount. Check: if the condition is worse than described in the decision, submit for review of the assessment (בדיקה מחדש) and to increase the level.
  5. Failure or level lower than necessary? Appeal - the deadline is indicated in the decision itself (standard 30 days). The chance is higher if you attach recent medical documents and a doctor’s report.

Bituach Leumi (National Insurance) phone numbers: *9696 from mobile, 04-8812345. Statements: btl.gov.il → “גמלת סיעוד”.

5

Siyud, “special services” benefit and insurance

Three different payments that are confusing

Siyud (Bituach Leumi) For older people (women - from 60 years old, men - from 67 years old). Hours of care or money. This page.
Benefit “special services” (שירותים מיוחדים) For people of working age with disabilities (at least 40%). Only with money. It is not assigned at the same time with a care benefit - one is selected. The maximum rate of this benefit in 2026 is 9,126 ₪.
Private long-term care insurance/health insurance (קופת חולים) Additional insurance (paid or included in the health insurance policy) - sums up with state support. If an older relative is already receiving coverage, check to see if they have unsolicited private insurance.

Questions and answers

Briefly about what is asked most often

How much do they pay for this in 2026?

By level: 1 - 1,705 ₪, 2 - 2,480 ₪, 3 - up to 4,216 ₪, 4 - up to 5,208 ₪, 5 - up to 6,448 ₪, 6 - up to 7,440 ₪ per month (amounts when hiring a permanent care worker, with 04/01/2026). Level 1 can be taken entirely with money.

Parents receive a pension of 15,000 ₪ - they will not be paid anything?

Optional: up to ₪20,654 (single) the payout is 50% of the level amount. And only pensions and old-age benefits are taken into account - not all income. Read from the table above.

Is it possible to hire a foreign care worker now?

Yes - this is one of the main routes to employment for a foreign care worker. The payment for a foreign worker is lower than for an Israeli one (table above). Admission is issued through Bituach Leumi and the Ministry of Social Welfare.

Level 3 was assigned, but the condition worsened. What to do?

Submit for review of assessment (בדיקה מחדש) - possible if the condition worsens at any levels 1–5. Attach recent extracts and a doctor's report. They review them periodically, upward.

Does Siyud replace a pension?

No. This is a separate payment that is assigned on top of other benefits, but with an income test: a high pension reduces it to 50% or cancels it. Pension - by right, siyud - according to the need for care.
