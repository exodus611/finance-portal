14 October 2026design

Extraordinary payments are now more compact: each payment is an expandable block

The “Extraordinary and one-off payments” section took up too much space: every payment was shown in full. Now each payment is an expandable block (accordion). Collapsed, you see the essentials: the name, the status with a date (“expected 28.10.2026 · in 14 days”), a short amount and who is entitled; one tap opens the details — when, to whom, for what, how to receive it, and a link to the source. The explanations under the list (early payments, arrears for the past, how to learn about new payments) have also become expandable. The section is several times shorter, especially on a phone. A link like payments.html#extra-heating-elderly opens the exact payment right away.

14 October 2026auto-update

Extraordinary payments now update themselves: amounts, dates and new Bituach Leumi announcements

The “Extraordinary and one-off payments” section no longer needs manual upkeep. Twice a day the site checks amounts and dates itself against the official Bituach Leumi pages (in January, when the amounts change, the page will update itself), reads new announcements and the “State of emergency” section, and adds new payments. A new payment is published only after a strict check: every figure and date must be found verbatim in the announcement text, and the link must lead only to the Bituach Leumi site. Such cards are marked “added automatically” with a link to the source. The cards themselves move through “expected → paid → next payment in such-and-such a month next year”; one-off payments hide themselves when the deadline has passed or the Bituach Leumi page has been deleted. Once a week the model's work is tested on a real sample: if something breaks, new payments are not published and the site keeps the last verified data.

14 October 2026payments

Bituach Leumi payments: a new section on extraordinary payments — to whom, when, how much and for what

A section “Extraordinary and one-off payments” has appeared on the payment-dates page: a heating grant for the elderly (664 ₪ per family, expected 28 October), a heating grant for victims of terror attacks (from 993 to 8,984 ₪, in November), a special payment for workers aged 67+ who lost their job because of the war (up to 137 ₪ per day, applications open), and a school grant (1,204 ₪ per child, paid 11 August). For each payment it says who is entitled, when it arrives, how much and for what, how to receive it, with a link to the official Bituach Leumi source; dates and amounts were checked on 14 October. The explanations on the page were also fixed: long-term benefits arrive on the 28th (not “around the 22nd”), unemployment around the 12th, and when a date falls on a Friday, Saturday or holiday, the money arrives earlier. The FAQ markup was fixed too: because of an extra character, search engines could not read it before.

27 September 2026fonts

The Inter font now lives on the site itself — pages look the same on all devices

The pages asked for the Inter font, but almost nowhere was it actually loaded: only six pages fetched it from the internet; the rest opened in whatever system font a person had — which is why the same site looked different on different phones and computers. Now Inter is connected locally, from our own domain: four files (Latin, extended Latin for French, Cyrillic, extended Cyrillic), and one file covers all weights from thin to extra-bold. There are no more requests to external services for the font — the page does not wait for someone else's servers and does not hand them visitor data. The external Inter connections were removed from tablo and valuta (and their French versions). Hebrew is deliberately left on the system font: it is used on the site for short terms, and the page weighs less.

17 September 2026explainer

Explained: ashlamat ahnasa and avtahat ahnasa are different payments

The two names sound alike, and readers confuse them constantly — yet their forms, thresholds and conditions differ. A comparison table was added: how the partial allowance (a top-up to the minimum on top of an already-granted pension, salary or unemployment) differs from the full one (for those with neither income nor the right to other payments), the 2026 amounts for both, the 24-month residence-in-Israel requirement for avtahat ahnasa, and a hint “how to quickly understand your case”. Both terms were added to the dictionary in Russian and French, and the Russian dictionary gained a “Bituach Leumi” category (it did not exist before — the Russian version of the dictionary lagged behind the French one).

17 September 2026verified against source

Savings and the supplement: the official Bituach Leumi mechanics from 01.01.2026 added

Before, the page only said “savings above the threshold — a check is needed”. Now the mechanics themselves are described and calculated in the calculator: on the amount above the exempt 41,528 ₪ (single) or 62,292 ₪ (couple), the institute computes a notional income at the average rate of 4.17% per year divided by 12; from 103,327 ₪ the calculation is tiered (family composition, age, size). Added: additional real estate — 3% for residential, 5% for commercial, with tiers above 292,931 ₪. Worked example: 100,000 ₪ for a single person → 203 ₪ of notional income per month.

How to read this changelog

  • How to tell you are seeing the fresh version. If a page looks stale, press Ctrl+F5 (on a phone — pull down to refresh, or open a private window): GitHub Pages caches files for up to 10 minutes. Then compare the date at the bottom of the page with the last entry of this changelog.
  • Who updates the site, and when. Part of the data updates automatically from official sources: the Bank of Israel rate, the consumer price index, the gasoline price, Bituach Leumi payment dates. The rest is done by hand; every change lands in this changelog with its date.
  • A remark or an error. If you see an outdated figure or an inaccuracy — write via the form: contacts. Corrections are logged here too.

The date of the last site change is at the bottom of every page. This changelog is updated together with the site.
