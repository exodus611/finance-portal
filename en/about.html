INDEPENDENT PROJECT

The numbers of life in Israel — honestly, openly, with no hidden commissions

We built the Clear Finance portal so that everyone in Israel — from a new immigrant to a lifelong resident — has a reliable, free tool to work out their own money, without banks or middlemen pushing products.

The project's 3 key principles

🔒

100% data privacy

Every calculator (salary, mashkanta, pension) runs entirely in your browser, on your device. Your income, savings and personal data are never sent anywhere or stored on servers.

🏛️

Official data gateways

We never invent numbers. Rates update directly from the Bank of Israel API, benefit dates from Bituach Leumi, inflation from the CBS, fuel prices from the Ministry of Energy.

⚖️

Complete independence

We are not a bank, broker or insurance agency, and there are no paid loan recommendations here. The goal is to give you an exact calculation so the decision stays yours.

Where our data comes from

  • Policy rate and Prime: The Bank of Israel open gateway (Bank of Israel API — GetInterest).
  • Consumer price index (Madad) and construction index: Central Bureau of Statistics (CBS API).
  • Payment dates and benefits: National Insurance Institute (Bituach Leumi — btl.gov.il).
  • Petrol 95 prices: Orders of the Fuel Administration of the government of Israel.
  • Fraud warnings: Israel National Cyber Directorate (Cyber Directorate).
