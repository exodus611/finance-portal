When is tax paid and when is it not?

The main rule that must be understood before making calculations.

✓ The first (only) apartment is often ZEROIf this is your only apartment in Israel, then the first 1,978,745 ₪ no tax is charged at all. Apartment for 1.8 million on the periphery - 0 ₪ tax.
But “first = free” is not always trueThe exemption is only valid up to ₪1,978,745. The average apartment in Israel costs around 2,330,000 ₪ (CSB, 2026) - this is above the threshold, and the tax will be about ₪12,294. In the center of the country, the first apartment is also taxed: for 2.5 million - 20,538 ₪, for 3.5 million - 70,538 ₪.
✗ Second apartment - paid from the first shekelNo exemption: 8% immediately. For the same average apartment 2,330,000 ₪ is 186,400 ₪ instead of 12,294 ₪ - the difference is 15 times.
Who doesn't pay at all?No one has a complete exemption in terms of cost, but there are zero rates: a single apartment up to 1,978,745 ₪ · a repatriate for takana 12א up to the same amount · a disabled person with a single apartment up to 2.5 million (for part up to the first level) · transfer of housing to a spouse living in the apartment (takana 21) - full exemption.

Conditions for the “single apartment” statusTo pay at preferential levels, you must fulfill all conditions: you are a resident of Israel (a foreign resident pays the rates of an additional apartment); this is your only apartment - a share of up to 1/3 in another or up to 1/2 in an inherited one does not deprive you of your status; the whole family is taken into account - you, your spouse and children under 18 years of age are considered one buyer; the object is housing, not a plot or commercial area; if you are improving your home, the old apartment is sold within the allotted time.

What is important to know

Terms and pitfalls.

Who is considered a “single buyer”

סעיף 9(ג)(2)(ב)(2) - the buyer, his spouse (except for a permanent resident) and children under 18 years of age are considered one buyer. Judicial practice has extended this rule to de facto spouses. If your spouse has an apartment, it is counted as yours.

When the apartment is still considered the only one
  • have a share no more than 1/3 in another apartment
  • have a share up to 1/2 in an inherited apartment
  • you improve your home and sell your old apartment on time

There are exceptions to these rules. If you have shares, be sure to consult with a real estate attorney.

Deadlines for improving housing - changed three times

Secondary market: from 06/01/2025 a constant norm applies - 24 months from the date of signing the contract. From 06/01/2023 to 05/31/2025, a temporary norm of 18 months was in force, it expired and was not extended.

From the developer: 12 months from the date on which possession was to be transferred under the contract. If there is a delay through no fault of yours - from the actual transfer.

Risk: A bill to return to 18 months is being promoted in the Knesset. Please check the current regulations before making a transaction.

Repatriates: the norm changed in August 2024

Valid until August 15, 2024 תקנה 12: 0.5% from the first shekel and 5% above the first step. It was unprofitable - the usual steps of a single apartment almost always gave less.

Introduced from 08/15/2024 תקנה 12א: for a single apartment 0% up to 1,978,745 ₪ and total 0.5% up to 6,055,070 ₪. This is significantly more profitable than conventional steps.

A repatriate whose repatriation day preceded the amendment has the right to choose the previous norm. The calculator considers both options.

Deadlines for submission and payment

Declaration in "Misuy Mekarkein" - within 30 days from signing the contract. Tax payment - within 60 days. Late payment incurs interest, indexation and possible fines. Typically, the declaration is submitted by the lawyer accompanying the transaction on the day of signing.

Non-residential real estate

תקנה 2(1) - 6% from the first shekel: plot, store, office, warehouse, as well as a house not suitable for habitation. When purchasing a residential plot, a 1/6 tax refund is possible if a building permit is obtained within 24 months.
