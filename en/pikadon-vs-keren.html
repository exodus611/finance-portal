The main thing is what is hidden behind the percentages

Three things that make the 4.5 vs 3.5 comparison mean nothing on its own

The tax is calculated from a different base - this is the whole trick
Tax in picadon 15% is taken from the entire accrued amount, regardless of inflation. In Money market fund the rate is higher - 25%, but they take it only from real profit, that is, from what remains over the growth of the consumer price index (מדד המחירים לצרכן).

Hence the rule: the higher the inflation, the more the fund wins. With inflation equal to the fund's return, the fund's tax is completely zeroed out - there is nothing to pay. And Deposit continues to pay 15% of each shekel of interest, even if this interest simply compensated for the rise in cost of living.

The opposite situation is deflation. If the index did not rise during the period, but fell, the benefit does not turn into a bonus: the law in this case considers all nominal profit to be real and taxes it at the same 25%. That is, a negative madad does not reduce the tax base below what is actually earned. The calculator takes this into account - try putting inflation with a minus sign.
Liquidity: picadon locks up money, fund doesn’t
Deposit is usually closed for a period of time. It is either impossible to leave earlier at all, or you can go to rare “exit stations” (תחנות יציאה), and then the interest is often lost in whole or in part.

Keren Caspit is sold on any trading day, money usually arrives within one business day, without penalties. If this is your safety net for a rainy day, a difference of two-tenths of a percent means much less than the ability to withdraw money tomorrow.
Guarantee vs. Appraisal: An Important Difference in Risk
Deposit rate fixed by agreement - the bank is obliged to pay it. Yield Money market fund not guaranteed: This is a valuation based on the fund holding short government securities (מק״מ) and deposits. If the Bank of Israel lowers the rate, the fund's profitability will go down along with it - and your already opened picadon will retain its interest.

The opposite is also true: if the rate rises, the fund will react quickly, and the picadon will remain on the old conditions until the end of the term. The risk of losing an investment in Money market fund is extremely low, but formally it is not zero and the deposit is not insured by the state like a bank deposit.
US citizens - special caution
For US tax residents, Israeli funds may be subject to PFIC treatment with heavy reporting and tax disadvantages in the US. In such a situation, a bank picadon often turns out to be simpler and cheaper, even with a lower profitability. This is the case when it is worth asking an accountant familiar with American reporting - the cost of an error here is higher than the difference in percentage.

This is an estimate and not a recommendation. Just so you don't get a false sense of precision, here's what the model simplifies.

We calculate using compound interest with annual capitalization, and a specific bank can charge differently - monthly, by “steps” or at a promotional rate only for the first period. Ask your bank for the actual formula.

Both rates are considered constant for the entire term. In real life, the Money market fund yield follows the Bank of Israel rate and changes even tomorrow, and inflation for the year rarely coincides with the forecast. On the contrary, your picadon bet is fixed - this is the difference in risk. Therefore, look not only at the result, but at the “breaking point” line: it shows how stable the conclusion is.

Fund tax is paid upon sale, and not annually - over long periods this gives a small additional benefit due to deferment, which the model does not consider. Also not taken into account are possible commissions on your brokerage account, the offset of losses against profits, and special tax benefits (for example, for low-income seniors under Section 125D).

Money market fund's profitability is not guaranteed. Before making a decision, check the actual conditions at the bank and the parameters of a particular fund - and if the amount is large, it makes sense to ask an accountant.
