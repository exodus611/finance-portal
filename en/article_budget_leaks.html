Where does the money flow: 4 invisible holes in the budget

Saving is not about giving up the joys of life, but about putting your bills in order. Here are four places where money is leaking away unnoticed, and what you can actually do about it.

1

Current account fees (עמלות)

The bank charges a small amount for almost any action: transferring money, paying by card, withdrawing cash. Individually it’s not noticeable, but in a month it’s a noticeable amount.

How to close: By law, every resident of Israel has the right to a basic account (חשבון בסיסי) is a special route where most standard commissions are not charged or they are symbolic. If you don't use complex banking products, it's worth asking your bank about switching to this route.
2

Dear communications and television

Many people have been paying for years for mobile communication tariffs and TV packages that were connected a long time ago and have never been reviewed - although the market for virtual operators and streaming has long offered cheaper prices.

How to close: Once a year, it’s worth checking your tariff with current offers on the market - communication prices in Israel change quickly, and what was profitable two years ago can today be noticeably more expensive than average.
3

Forgotten regular payments (הוראות קבע)

An annual gym membership, an old app subscription, a one-time donation turned monthly—recurring payments are easy to forget because they don’t require re-approval every time.

How to close: A banking app usually has a separate section for recurring payments (הוראות קבע) - once every few months it’s worth opening it and checking whether you really use all of it.
4

Electricity (חשמל) at the default rate

Israel operates a mechanism for alternative electricity suppliers (ספקי חשמל פרטיים): electricity physically runs through the same wires, but the bill is issued by a private company at a discount to the government tariff.

How to close: to connect an alternative supplier, you usually need a smart meter (מונה חכם). If you have an old meter, you can ask the state electric company about replacing it.
⚠️ The size of the discount differs from different suppliers and changes over time - compare current offers yourself, and do not rely on numbers from old articles.
Four holes higher in total is common several hundred ₪ per month, which can be returned without a single day of austerity.
