Why liberation is not “all or nothing”

If a single apartment is sold above the ceiling (5,008,000 ₪), not the entire profit is released, but only a proportional part - that share of the profit that relates to the price up to the ceiling. The portion above the ceiling is taxed as usual. Many calculators on the Internet mistakenly consider this to be "all or nothing" - this is corrected here.

What is linear preferential calculation (חישוב ליניארי מוטב)

For apartments purchased before January 1, 2014, profits are divided proportionally according to the time of ownership: the portion accumulated before 2014 is completely exempt from tax - regardless of the status of the only apartment. Only part of the profit accumulated after 2014 is taxed (and the exemption ceiling is already applied to it if it is the only apartment).

What is extra tax (מס יסף)

Additional tax of 5% for those whose total annual income exceeds a certain threshold. Can increase the effective rate from 25% to 30% on a portion of the profit. The exact calculation depends on your total annual income for the year of sale - the calculator only warns about the risk, it does not calculate the exact amount.

Real profit and indexation

By law, the profit is considered “real” - that is, the purchase price is conditionally tied to the consumer price index on the day of purchase. For simplicity, this calculator compares prices directly, without indexation - if inflation is high over a long period of ownership, the exact amount from the tax office may differ slightly.

The calculation is of an estimation nature according to the general methodology of the law and does not constitute tax or legal advice. Not taken into account: exact indexation to madad, hereditary and family situations, benefits for two apartments during the transition period, regional benefits. Before the transaction, contact a real estate lawyer or tax consultant - the exact amount is determined by Misui Mekarkein based on the submitted declaration.
