📌 Pension consultant's conclusion: Delaying exit from an early age (60–63) until the official one gives a quadruple benefit: Kibua Zhuyot (0 tax), 0 ₪ Bituach Leumi contributions and health tax, state old age benefit "Ezrach Vatik" and reduction in mikadem.
📖 Official Rules: Taxation of Pensions, Kibuah Zhuyot and Bituach Leumi (National Insurance) Contributions (Art. 345b, Art. 9a)
  1. Contributions to Bituach Leumi and Healthcare (Article 345b of the National Insurance Law):
    • For early exit (60–66 years): pension fund by law (סעיף 345ב לחוק הביטוח הלאומי) is obliged to withhold contributions for national insurance and health care tax from the pension (3.49% up to 7,522 ₪ and 11.79% above).
    • From official age (67 years for men / 62–65 for women): comes 100% exemption from Bituach Leumi contributions and health tax from the fund's pension payments. Medical insurance (~223 ₪) is deducted by the state from the state old-age benefit, and you receive the fund's pension without deductions!
  2. Income tax (Mas Akhnasa - art. 9a):
    • Before official age: the pension is taxed as ordinary income without applying the Kibua Zhuyot tax basket.
    • From official age: the pensioner fills out form 161d (*טופס 161ד*) and receives a tax-free basket up to 4,950 ₪/month, reducing income tax to 0 ₪.
  3. Rule of 32 years (נוסחת השילוב): The tax office checks severance payments (pitsuim) taken without tax over the last 32 years. Every 1 ₪ taken from pitsuim “eats” 1.35 ₪ from the preferential capital.
