How to translate an official website into Russian

Method 1 - Chrome/Edge

Open the official website → right click → “Translate into Russian”. On the phone: menu ⋮ → Translate.

Method 2 – Safari on iPhone/iPad

Open the official website → in the address bar, click the button AA → "Translate into Russian". Google closed automatic translation of Hebrew languages ​​in 2025. sites through links - the browser’s built-in translation works more reliably.

Method 3 - neuron according to the screenshot

Take a screenshot of the official page and ask ChatGPT/Gemini/Claude to explain the interface. Do not send documents, ID numbers, bank details, passwords or SMS codes.

Method 4 - Google Lens on your phone

Take a screenshot → open Google Lens → select “Translate” → Russian language. This is convenient if the site is not translated automatically.

If you need a mortgage consultant

For יועץ משכנתאות there is no such simple official public registry of licenses found as for attorneys, CPAs, brokers, insurance or investment professionals. Therefore, it is more useful here not to “check the license”, but to have a list of questions before starting work.

Ask for a written contract Check if he receives a commission from the bank Ask how many banks compare Ask for a rate growth scenario Check the total cost of the loan, not just the payment Check if there is professional insurance

Pension planning vs retirement

Here's a collection of ALL the similar titles you'll come across when looking for a retirement professional - and clearly states which ones have a real, verifiable state license and which ones don't.

Main trap: “יועץ פנסיוני” (pension consultant) and “יועץ פרישה” (retirement consultant) sound almost the same in Russian, but the first is a real license, and the second is not at all.

How to read this table: the first four lines (without yellow shading) are licenses that are actually checked for gov.il. The last three lines (with yellow shading) are titles that sound professional, but are NOT government licensed or verified anywhere. A person with such a title may ADDITIONALLY have one of the real licenses above - it's worth asking directly.

Three real licenses - what each one specifically allows

All three are taught the same way and the exam is the same. The difference is not in knowledge, but in what exactly the law allows and prohibits for each type of license.

The result in simple words: a consultant is someone who will tell you objectively what to do, but will not be able to formalize it himself. An agent is someone who can arrange, but selects from a limited set of companies and receives a commission from this. A marketer is an employee of one specific company, offering only its products. Consultant and agent - mutually exclusive licenses, you cannot hold both at the same time: a consultant, by definition, is prohibited from any connection with companies, and an agent, by definition, works under a contract with them.

Rule of thumb: if a person gives advice on pension products, check the license in the Republic of Lithuania. If talking about taxes in retirement - check with a CPA/tax advisor. If it advises investments, check the ISA license.
