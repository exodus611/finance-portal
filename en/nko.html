🤝 Israeli Charitable Organizations

Large verified NPOs (amutot) that really help - and an official way to check any organization on your own, even one that is not on our list.

This is an informational guide and is not a solicitation for donations or advertising. We do not take or give money, we are not an intermediary. All links are to the official websites of organizations and the state register.

💰 It’s about money - not about food

The only organization on this list that specifically deals with financial literacy and family budgeting—not with food or holiday packages.

🔔Paamonimפעמונים▸
Amuta number: 580392702 (“פעמונים אירגון חסד”)

What he does: free financial counseling for any family or individual - help with budgeting, debt management, financial literacy. Not just for the “very poor” - the service is open to anyone who wants to sort out their money.

How it works: a one-time consultation (3 meetings) for those who want to quickly get the tools and figure it out on their own, or long-term personal or group support for several months for more complex cases. They also conduct lectures and have their own application for budget control.

Scale: have helped tens of thousands of families in Israel, work throughout the country, consultations can be obtained remotely (Zoom).

Five large organizations - where to start

Independently verified (scale, details) - not only according to the organizations themselves.

🍞Latetלתת▸
The largest in terms of donations in Israel

What he does: combating poverty and food insecurity - food assistance, educational and social programs for needy families throughout the country.

Scale: according to independent data (Wikipedia, donation reports) - the largest recipient of private donations in Israel, about 94 million ₪ for the reporting year.

Official website › Check in Guidestar ›
🤲Yad Eliezerיד אליעזר▸
Amuta No.: 580060069

What he does: food certificates and packages for needy families on an ongoing basis (not only for holidays), assistance to newlyweds from low-income families, hot lunches.

Scale: 40 years of work, about 130,000 people receive assistance monthly (data from the organization itself).

Official website › Check in Guidestar ›
🥕Leket Israelלקט ישראל▸

What he does: rescuing surplus food (from the fields, from restaurants and events) and transferring it through a network of partner amutos throughout the country - this is an “umbrella” organization that does not directly issue assistance itself, but directs it to the nearest partner amutot in your area.

How to get help: fill out the form on the website - they will select the nearest partner organization for the region (center, north, south, Sharon, Jerusalem and the surrounding area, Judea and Samaria).

Scale: about 250,000 people in need per year, about 6,000 food packages per month (organization data).

🔍 How to check any other organization yourself

There are about 16,000 operating amutot in Israel - we physically cannot list them all. But there is an official state instrument where there is all, including small and local:

🏛️Guidestar Israelגיידסטאר▸

The official Ministry of Justice site with data on 42,000+ amutot, halatz (public-benefit companies) and foundations in Israel — from the official registry, not manually compiled.

What to see there:

Amut number (misPar amut)מספר עמותה
Ishur nihul takin – Certificate of good financial managementאישור ניהול תקין
Article 46 — tax benefit for the donor (a sign that finances have been verified)אישור סעיף 46

How to search: enter the name of the organization in Hebrew (or at least part of it) into the search bar on the website. If an organization has a valid “ishur nihul takin”, this is a good sign: it means that the reporting has been checked and is in order.
