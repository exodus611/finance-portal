What is mekadem and why is it about 200

Mekadem Hamara (מקדם המרה) is a divisor that converts accumulated capital into a monthly payment. Capital 2,000,000 ₪ with mekadem 200 gives 10,000 ₪ per month. Depends on life expectancy, gender and age at exit, and is finally fixed only on the day of retirement. For women it is higher (longer payments), so with equal capital the pension is smaller.

Why is it so expensive to withdraw compensation for dismissal?

Compensation for dismissal (פיצויים) is 6–8.33% of the salary, that is, approximately one third of all pension contributions. When you leave, you can take them in cash, and many do this. But this money had to work for decades. Withdrawing ₪100,000 at age 40 is minus ₪250,000–400,000 capital by age 67.

Where does kitzvat zikna come from?

Basic payment of Bituach Leumi (National Insurance) in 2026: 1,838 ₪ lonely, 2,762 ₪ if the spouse does not have his own. For each year of insurance over ten, 2% is added, maximum +50%. This is the first level of pension, and it alone does not provide a living wage.

Why my calculation may differ from the fund's calculation

We calculate in today's money: we subtract inflation from the nominal yield, getting the real one. Funds often feature nominal amounts that appear larger but have lower purchasing power. Our approach is more conservative and honest.

What to do if the gap is large

In terms of efficiency: maintain compensation for dismissal when changing jobs · increase your share of contributions to 7% (this is also a tax benefit) · open a fund for advanced training (קרן השתלמות), if the employer agrees · consider delaying retirement - each year adds both savings and +5% to the kitsva · reduce fund commissions.

The calculation is an estimate and does not constitute pension or investment advice. Fund returns are a guess, not a guarantee. Mekadem is fixed only on the day of retirement. Not taken into account: taxation of pensions, insurance risks, training fund and private savings. For a personalized plan, consult a licensed retirement advisor.
