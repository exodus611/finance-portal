What it looks like next to Europe

Data from the European Commission Weekly Fuel Bulletin. Updated once a week, on Thursdays.

Data on July 27, 2026. Conversion according to the exchange rate 3.5133 shekel per euro.

Of the twenty-seven countries of the European Union, refueling in twenty-five is cheaper than in Israel. More expensive only in Denmark and the Netherlands.

Our reason is the same as that of expensive European countries: high taxes on fuel. Where it is cheaper to refuel, the excise tax is lower, and not the cost of the oil itself - everyone buys the raw material at approximately the same price.

What moves the price

The new price is announced two days before the end of the month. It depends on three things.

We deliberately do not give a forecast. The price depends on five trading days at the end of the month, and during this time the quotes have time to reverse: at the end of July, oil fell in price from $105 to $92 in four days. There are only facts here, and the conclusions are yours.

This does not apply to diesel fuel.

The state sets the maximum price only for gasoline ninety-five. The Ministry of Energy does not regulate diesel fuel: the price is set by the fuel companies themselves, and it differs noticeably at different gas stations. Many people think that the advertised price applies to her.
