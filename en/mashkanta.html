🟢 1. Maximum payment reduction (Extend for 30 years)
🎯 For whom: Families for whom it is critical to reduce their monthly payment right now.
📖 How it works: All tracks stretch for a maximum period of 30 years.
💰 Result: The payment is reduced by ~-1,250 ₪/month compared to 25 years.
🔵 2. FIRE: Interest reduction (CALATS 15 years at 4.1%)
🎯 For whom: For those who are willing to pay a little more to save on interest.
📖 How it works: We reduce the KALATS to 15 years - the bank gives a lower rate.
💰 Result: Saving more than 250,000 ₪ percent.
🟡 3. Golden mean 50/50
🎯 For whom: The ideal compromise between current load and savings.
📖 How it works: Prime 28 years, KALATS 20 years (4.6%), Mishtana 25 years.
💰 Result: -500 ₪/month and ~115,000 ₪ savings.
🟢 4. Grace period for 2 years
🎯 For whom: When buying "on paper" or renovating — paying both rent and a mortgage.
📖 How it works: The first 24 months are interest only, no debt.
💰 Result: Payment drops to ~5,478 ₪/month for 2 years.
🟢 5. Prime-Maximum 50%
🎯 For whom: Who is counting on the Central Bank rate cut?
📖 How it works: Prime 50% (maximum), KALATS 35%, Mishtana 15%.
💰 Result: The payment falls on its own with each reduction in the Central Bank rate.
🔵 6. Keren Shava (Equal repayment)
🎯 For whom: Borrowers with good income.
📖 How it works: The body of the debt is divided equally, interest is divided into the remainder.
💰 Result: Save up to 320,000 ₪!
🟣 7. Stepped Tamahil (15/20/25)
🎯 For whom: Families with rising costs in the future.
📖 How it works: Mishtana 15 years, KALATS 20 years, Prime 25 years.
💰 Result: Payment drops by 1.3 times every 5 years.
🔴 8. 100% Inflation Protection
🎯 For whom: Who wants 100% predictability.
📖 How it works: No price index. 50% Prime + 50% CALAC.
💰 Result: Body of debt never grows.
