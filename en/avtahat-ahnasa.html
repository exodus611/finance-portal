Subsistence allowance (avtahat akhnasa) - verification of eligibility and amount 2026 הבטחת הכנסה · full benefit when there is no income or right to other benefits

Amounts of Bituach Leumi (National Insurance) · 2026 Avtakhat akhnasa - separate benefit, and not a pension supplement. It is paid to those who do not have income from work and are not entitled to other payments: for illness, disability, injury at work, long-term unemployment. A single person aged 25–54 gets 2 076 ₪ per month. Single parent 55+ with two children - up to 5 289 ₪. The main barrier for new repatriates is 24 months residency, but this rule has exceptions.

This is a guideline, not a decision of Bituach Leumi. The amount depends on age, family composition, income, property and registration with the employment service. The institute makes the exact calculation - it has official calculator. The numbers on the page are the Bituach Leumi table, valid from 01/01/2026.

Residence: 24 months and five exceptions

The benefit is available to an Israeli resident with experience at least 24 consecutive months. On this basis, those who arrived a year or two ago are most often denied: the department hears “no 24 months” - and the conversation ends.

But the rule is not absolute. Eligibility is tested with exceptions if the person or his spouse meets at least one condition.

Employment service: without registration there will be no benefits

You need to register with the employment service (לשכת התעסוקה) and appear as a job seeker - you and your spouse, if you have one. You can sign up online, but you need to come to the branch within 15 days; The days of attendance are counted from the date of online registration.

Not only the unemployed are registered: those who work for low wages; students of professional courses - service direction, full-time study, no more than 12 months; single parents who study; self-employed with low income and starting a business.

Single parents-students pay up to 36 months if the benefit was already 16 months out of 20 before the start of studies. Exempt from appearing in some cases, for example upon reaching retirement age. But liberation must be received, not assumed.

For a couple, the income of both spouses is taken into account, and both are checked for all conditions. If the spouse is not a resident of Israel, 779 ₪ will be deducted from the benefit.

What is deducted and what is given in excess of money

Benefit deductions: 123 ₪ - minimum contributions for health insurance if a person does not work; 779 ₪ - if the spouse is not a resident of Israel or is fully supported by the state, the Council or the municipality.

Surcharge: women born between 1960 and 1964 receive 825 ₪ from the month they turned 62 until retirement age. This will compensate for raising the retirement age from 62 to 65 years.

Beyond money benefit recipients are included in the preferential lists - this is often more than the payment itself. Check at your branch and at the city hall: discount on Arnona, benefits on transport, medicine and telefee, assistance with rent.

How to apply

  1. Select a form. If you do not receive an old-age or survivors pension, this is בל/5619, application for subsistence benefits. If the pension has already been assigned - בל/430, and then it's about pension supplement.
  2. Register with the employment service online and come to the department within 15 days: without appearing, your eligibility will not be checked.
  3. Collect documents: bank statements for 12 months; confirmation of income from all sources – your own and your spouse’s; confirmation of property value; if work has stopped or been reduced - confirmation from the employer.
  4. Submit your application to the Bituach Leumi branch. The right arises from the month of filing the application.
  5. Ask for retroactive payment, if the right was earlier: those who have already reached retirement age can be assigned it up to 12 months ago; The survivor pension supplement is paid for a period of up to 6 months before filing. Retroactive benefits are not given.
  6. Report changes: income, family composition, address - within 30 days. The benefit arrives on the 12th for the previous month; dates including holidays - in payment calendar.

Frequently asked questions

I am 32 years old, I repatriated a year ago. Am I entitled to benefits?

The general rule is 24 months of residency, and the department will most likely refuse you based on this. But repatriates have an exception: the right is checked if you have lived in the country for 12 months - not necessarily consecutive - and no more than 5 years have passed since aliyah. Plus, in the first months after the end of “sal klit”, support from the Ministry of Aliyah (“avtahat kiyum”) is possible: it is given to pregnant women, the sick, those caring for a sick relative, single parents and repatriates with disabilities. The right step is to submit an application and attach documents for aliyah, and not turn around after the first “no”.

I already receive an old-age pension. Am I paid avtahat akhnasa?

After retirement age, the autahat akhnas table does not apply: they pay the same amount as the old-age pension with a social supplement - from 4,375 ₪ for a single person and 6,912 ₪ for a couple up to 70 years old. Formally, this is the same law, but a different article; we have about her calculator page.

I have my own apartment and car - does this interfere?

The apartment you live in is not taken into account - additional real estate is considered. A car up to ₪46,138 does not interfere with your rights. In the range of 46,138 - 65,343 ₪, people of retirement age, survivors' pension recipients and family members of those killed at work retain the right, while for others the calculated amount is withheld from the benefit.

How much savings can you have?

Up to 41,528 ₪ for a single person and up to 62,292 ₪ for a couple - does not affect eligibility. From the amount above the threshold, conditional income is calculated at a rate of 4.17% per annum ÷ 12, and from ₪ 117,173 steps are included. Guideline: 100,000 ₪ gives a single person about 203 ₪ of conditional income per month - this in itself does not deprive you of benefits.

From what month will I be paid if I submit an application today?

The right arises from the month of filing the application. For those who have already reached retirement age, retroactive payment is possible - up to 12 months; The survivor pension supplement is paid for a period of up to 6 months before filing. Therefore, it is worth submitting an application even if in doubt: in the worst case, you will receive a refusal with a reason, and it can be contested.
