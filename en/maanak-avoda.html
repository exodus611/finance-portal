Why is the government paying this money?

Economic meaning of the Negative Income Tax Law

In 2007, the Knesset passed a special law (חוק להגדלת שיעור ההשתתפות בכוח העבודה ולצמצום פערים). The main goal of the state is encourage people to work rather than sit on welfare.
If a person gets a part-time job or a low salary, the state directly pays him a bonus into his bank account. This makes going to work much more beneficial for the family budget than living solely on income support benefits (הבטחת הכנסה).

IMPORTANT: 4 facts that many people don’t know

💰 This is not a poverty benefit

This is a state bonus for being are you working. About 300,000 Israelis lose thousands of shekels every year simply because they don't check their eligibility.

🚫 Be careful with intermediaries

Don’t give “helpers” 20–30% of the commission. Submission on the official website רשות המסים It's absolutely free and takes exactly 3 minutes.

⏳ Money for 2024 is burned out

The law gives exactly 2 years. If you do not apply for the 2024 work year before December 31, 2026, this money will forever be burned in favor of the state.

👥 Each spouse has their own form

Each working spouse submits your personal statement under your terms of reference. Incomes are not summed up: if both work, everyone receives their own payment!

ℹ️ Rule for singles without children (יחיד ללא ילדים):
According to Israeli Law, citizens without minor children have the right to Maanak Avodah strictly starting from age 55 years. If you are under 55 years old, payment is due only if you have at least 1 child under 19 years old.
👥 Rule for spouses (בני זוג):
Each working spouse calculates the payment according to your personal income and serves its own separate statement from your personal account under your Teudat Zehut. Income of spouses do not add up: if both spouses work and both meet the criteria, the family receives two payments! In the questionnaire, the spouse’s details are indicated solely to verify the absence of a second property (>50%) and reconciliation of children.

How the tax office checks reports: forms 106, 126 and 1301

Why do payments start only in July and not immediately in January?

In Israel, taxation is considered total for the full calendar year (from January 1 to December 31). Therefore, the data verification process looks like this:
1. Employee’s annual report (טופס 106)
Issued until March 31st
The employer is obliged to prepare and issue to the employee a final annual report on all accruals (טופס 106) until March 31 of the following year.
2. Employer’s report to the tax office (טופס 126)
Available until April 30
The employer centrally transmits a digital report on all employees (טופס 126) to the Tax Office (רשות המסים).
3. Automatic database reconciliation
May – June
The IRS automatically matches the claim you submit with 126 reports from all of your employers and calculates the exact payment amount.
4. First transfer to a bank account
July 15
That is why the first payment for last year is transferred July 15 — when all employers in the country have already submitted their annual reports.

* Important: apply online for gov.il possible as early as January. Your application will be saved in the system and will be automatically calculated as soon as the employer submits the report.

How to apply online in 3 minutes

Free on the state portal without intermediaries

1. Go to the official portal of the Tax Administration: gov.il/he/service/submitting-an-online-claim-for-job-grant.
2. Log in through the government identification system (מערכת ההזדהות הלאומית) by ID number (תעודת זהות).
3. Provide your bank account details (bank, branch, account number).
4. For hired workers: no certificates or salary slips (תלושי שכר) attach no need — the tax service checks income automatically using reports 106/126 from employers.
5. Individual entrepreneurs (עצמאים): Before submitting, make sure you have submitted your annual report (דוח שנתי - טופס 1301) for the audited year.
6. Confirm shipment. The review status can be tracked in your personal account.
⚠️ Why are those who have a share in their parents’ apartment denied? ▸
This is the most common reason for refusal. Article 3 of the Law clearly defines: if the applicant or his spouse has more than 50% share in any real estate, except for the only apartment for your own residence, the right to payment is canceled completely (0 ₪). This applies to inherited shares, commercial warehouses, shops and development land.
👶 What is the allowance for children (מענק לפעוטות)? ▸
This is a special government subsidy for toddlers. Working parents of older children up to 3 years receive an additional increase to Maanak Avodah in the amount of up to 470 – 940 ₪ per month for each child. Our calculator automatically takes this surcharge into account when calculating.
