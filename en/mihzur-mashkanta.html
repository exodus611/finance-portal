What is the penalty for early repayment (עמלת היוון)
This is compensation to the bank for lost interest - the largest component of the fine. It only occurs if your bet higher current market average. If your rate is lower than the market rate, your compensation is zero. For the “Prime” and “variable once a year” channels, it is not charged at all - they can be repaid freely.
Where does the fine discount come from?
According to a bank order for early repayment (צו הבנקאות) the bank is obliged to reduce compensation depending on the age of the loan: 3–5 years - 20% discount, over 5 years - 30%. For preferential mortgage (תעודת זכאות) discounts are higher: from 10% after a year to 40% after four years.
How to avoid unnecessary fees
Notify the bank in advance (usually at least 10 days in advance) - this can save you a separate fee for not notifying. A small transaction fee still applies. The bank is obliged upon request to provide a free written calculation of the fine (דף אסבר) - request it before making a decision; check the exact conditions and frequency of free requests with the bank.
How accurate is the calculation?
This is an assessment based on a general methodology using average market rates. The bank calculates according to the actual yield curve on the maturity date, so the final amount may differ. Use the result to understand the order of the numbers and decide whether to go to the bank for an accurate calculation.
