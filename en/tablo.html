Frequently asked questions

What is prime and where does it come from?

Prime is the Bank of Israel rate plus 1.5 percentage points. Banks use it as a basis for loans and mortgages. When the Bank of Israel changes the rate, the prime rate changes automatically on the same day.

What does “the price is tied to madad” mean?

This means that the amount is recalculated together with the consumer price index. Most often, this is how rental housing and part of the mortgage tracks work. The index rose by 2% - the amount will also increase by about 2%.

How does the official rate differ from the market rate?

The official one is published by the Bank of Israel on weekdays around 9:20, and it is valid all day - taxes and customs are calculated using it. The market changes constantly and is closer to what you will see in the exchanger. Both are shown above.

When is the next bet decision?

The Bank of Israel makes decisions about eight times a year. The nearest date is indicated on the tile with the bet.

Where does the data come from?

From the open APIs of the Bank of Israel, the Central Bureau of Statistics and the Government Open Data Portal. Links to all sources are at the bottom of the page.
