How does this work

Without illusions and naive comparisons.

Why can't you just compare 4.9% and 7%?
Investment income is taxable, but interest savings on mashkant are not. With a 25% tax, a 7% return turns into about 5.25% net. The gap is reduced threefold. That is why repaying the mortgage often turns out to be more reasonable than it seems at first glance.
Why do we consider future value and not “interest saved”
A naive comparison forgets that after repayment the monthly payment is released and can also be invested. We compare two scenarios with the same cash flows: in both, you spend the same amount now and the same amount each month. The only difference is where this money goes.
What is the point of indifference
The yield at which both options give the same result. If you believe that the market will give more, it is more profitable to invest. If less, repay. This is not a forecast, but a guide for your own decision.
Indexed channels (צמוד מדד)
With a chain channel, the real cost of debt is equal to the interest rate plus inflation. A channel at 3.3% with a madad of 1.6% actually costs about 4.9% - more expensive than it looks. Therefore, chain channels are often more profitable to repay first, even if the nominal rate is lower.
Reduce payment or shorten term
Shortening the term saves more interest, but the money “works” inside the loan at its rate. Reducing your payment frees up the money immediately so it can be invested. The calculator considers both options and tells you which one is better given your numbers.
