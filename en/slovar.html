Glossary of terms

What does the bank say -
and what does it really mean

Each term: how it sounds in the bank’s document → in simple words → how much it changes in shekels using a specific example. We do not advise - we only explain the mechanism.

Bituach Leumi

Ashlamat akhnasa (השלמת הכנסה)

In a letter from Bituach Leumi: “אושרה לך תוספת השלמת הכנסה לקצבת אזרח ותיק.”
HumanlyThis social pension supplement: The institute pays the difference between your pension and the minimum established for your family composition and age. Pay partial - it complements what is already there: old-age pension, survivor's pension, salary or unemployment benefit.
How much is this in shekels2026: single - income increased to 4 375 / 4 418 / 4 460 ₪ (up to 70 / 70–80 / 80+); couple - up to 6 912 / 6 981 / 7 048 ₪. The pension can be converted into social allowance calculator.
What's importantNot to be confused with autahat akhnasa — there is a different form, different thresholds and a requirement of 24 months of residence in Israel.
Bituach Leumi

Avtahat akhnasa (הבטחת הכנסה)

In the Bituach Leumi branch: “You cannot apply for הבטחת הכנסה, apply for השלמת הכנסה.”
HumanlyComplete subsistence allowance - for those who have neither income nor the right to other payments: illness, disability, work injury, long-term unemployment. Age: from 20 years until retirement age, and after it - if a pension is not assigned.
How much is this in shekels2026: lonely - 2 076 ₪ at 25–54 years old and 2 596 ₪ from 55 years to retirement age; couple - 2,855 ₪ and 3,893 ₪ respectively.
What's importantThe residence requirement is stricter than the pension supplement: no less 24 months continuous residence in Israel. It is this condition that is most often denied to new repatriates. Amounts by age and family composition, form 5619 and exceptions for repatriates - on the page autahat akhnasa.
Bank and account

Overdraft

The bank says: “You have a credit limit available on your current account.”
HumanlyThis is permission to go into the red according to the usual account. Formally, it’s the same loan, only without a separate agreement: the bank simply allows the account to become negative up to the agreed limit and takes interest for this for each day.
How much is this in shekelsWith a minus of 5,000 ₪ and a rate of about 10–12% per annum, this is approximately 40–50 ₪ per month only interest, even if the amount does not change.
⚠️ The catch: the overdraft rate is usually noticeably higher than on a regular consumer loan - this is the most expensive way to borrow money from your own bank.
Bank and account

Current account (חשבון עו״ש)

The bank says: “Account maintenance is N ₪ per month.”
HumanlyA regular current account for salaries and payments. Separately from it - commissions for service, transfers, cash withdrawals over the limit, paper statements. Their size differs for different banks and different service packages.
How much is this in shekelsCommission for maintaining an account - usually from 0 ₪ (for some banks and packages) to 15–25 ₪/month, that is, 180–300 ₪ per year for the mere fact of having an account.
Mortgage

Prime Bet (ריבית פריים)

The bank says: “Track Prime minus 0.5%” or “Prime plus 1.2%.”
HumanlyPrime is the Bank of Israel's base rate plus a fixed surcharge of 1.5%. When the central bank changes its rate, your Prime track payment changes along with it - up or down, automatically, without a new contract.
How much is this in shekelsIf the debt balance is ₪500,000, the rate changes to +1% ≈ +400–420 ₪ to monthly payments until the track is paid off or refinanced.
⚠️ The catch: a low rate “today” says nothing about the rate in 3 years - the Prime track exists to move with the market.
Mortgage

Indexing / Madad (צמוד מדד)

The bank says: “Track linked to the consumer price index.”
HumanlyThe debt balance itself grows with inflation - even if you didn’t take a shekel from above and pay regularly. Prices in the country have increased, and the amount you owe to the bank has also increased.
How much is this in shekelsWith a debt of 400,000 ₪ and inflation of 3% per year, the debt body grows by ≈12 000 ₪ a year before you even start paying it off.
Mortgage

Effective interest rate

The contract states “nominal rate”, and next to it in small print - “effective”.
HumanlyThe nominal rate is like a “price per year”. Effective - what actually accumulates, taking into account how often interest is calculated and capitalized. The effective rate is almost always slightly higher than the nominal rate - it is according to this that you need to compare offers.
How much is this in shekelsA difference of tenths of a percent in the amount of ₪500,000 over 20 years is often 10 000–20 000 ₪ additional overpayment, invisible in the advertising booklet.
Salary

Tax points / Nekudot zikuy (נקודות זיכוי)

In the calculation sheet there is a line: “Head: 2.25”.
HumanlyThis is not a discount or a bonus, but a standard non-taxable minimum, which is due to almost everyone (plus additional points - for children, repatriation, single parenthood, etc.). Each point reduces your income tax by a fixed amount per month.
How much is this in shekelsOne tax point in 2026 is approximately 242 ₪ tax savings per month, that is, almost 2,900 ₪ per year for one single point.
⚠️ The catch: if the points are not indicated to the employer (for example, when changing jobs or for new repatriates), the tax is withheld to the maximum - the overpayment can only be returned through a separate request to the tax office.
Salary

Fund for Advanced Studies (קרן השתלמות)

On the payslip: “Fund for advanced training” or abbreviated as “קה״ש”.
HumanlyThis is not a tax or an expense - the money is not spent, but accumulated in a separate account and generates profitability, that is, over time the amount grows and does not decrease. The employer contributes about three times as much as the employee, and that portion is not considered your taxable income. Not everyone has it - only if it is specified in the employment contract.
Typical proportions and main benefitWorker - 2,5%, employer - 7,5% (1 to 3). If you don't take the money first 6 years, the entire amount - including all income - can be withdrawn in full without capital gains tax (usually 25%).
⚠️ The annual contribution for the full benefit is limited by a ceiling (≈15,712 ₪ per year for an employee, 2026) - beyond this the benefit does not apply.
Salary

Pension contributions - not from the entire amount (הפרשות לפנסיה)

The pay slip shows one percentage, but a glance at the amount of overtime raises a question.
HumanlyPension interest is usually calculated from the base salary, and not from the total amount payable. Payment for overtime and part of one-time bonuses are often NOT included in the base for pension contributions.
What does this mean in practice?If you had extra hours and your pension benefits were not deducted from them, this is usually not a mistake accounting, but a standard rule for most contracts.
Salary

National insurance and health care contribution (ביטוח לאומי, מס בריאות)

Two separate deductions on a payslip are often confused with each other.
HumanlyBituach Leumi (ביטוח לאומי, National Insurance Institute) - old-age benefits, unemployment benefits and injuries at work, maternity benefits. Mas Briut (מס בריאות) - a separate contribution specifically for health insurance (Kupat Holim). It is not the same tax under two names.
Accurate rates for 2026Up to 7,703 ₪ salary - reduced rate: 1,04% Bituach Leumi (National Insurance) + 3,23% healthcare. Above the threshold and up to the ceiling 51,910 ₪ - full rate: 7% + 5,17%. Example: gross 12,000 ₪ → ≈381 ₪ + ≈471 ₪ ≈ 852 ₪ per month.
Salary

Tax reconciliation for two jobs (תיאום מס)

Relevant for those who have more than one employer at the same time.
HumanlyWithout agreement, each employer applies the reduced tax rate and credit points from scratch as if it were your only job. In total, more tax is withheld from two places than is required based on the total annual income.
What to doDesign thium mas at the tax office (in person or online through Rashut HaMisim) - benefits and a reduced rate are applied only in one place, the second is immediately calculated at the full rate without doubling.
⚠️ Without approval, the overpayment is not lost forever - but it can only be returned later, through an annual report to the tax office, and all this time the money simply lies with the state, not with you. The same applies to the Bituach Leumi (National Insurance) threshold - it also needs to be distributed among employers.
Automobile

Notional cost of capital

In the calculator, the line with an asterisk: “Conditional cost of capital*”.
HumanlyNot a payment or a bank account. This is the cost of having the money invested in the car rather than sitting on deposit and not working otherwise - a missed opportunity rather than a real out-of-pocket expense this month.
How much is this in shekelsWith 75,000 ₪ invested in a car, under conditional 6.5% per annum ≈ 400 ₪/month - this is not what you pay, but what you theoretically lose.
Automobile

Large residual payment / “Ballon” (תשלום בלון)

In the leasing agreement: “The monthly payment is lower due to the residual value at the end.”
HumanlyPart of the car price is deferred until the very end of the contract. The monthly payment seems lower because this share is not paid off yet - but it will still have to be paid, refinanced, or closed by selling the car.
How much is this in shekelsWith a remaining 40% of the price of 150,000 ₪, this is 60 000 ₪, which are waiting for you at the end of the term in one amount.
⚠️ The catch: this is not a discount on the car, but simply a part of the price transferred in time - and interest may also accrue on it.
