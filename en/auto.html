Why are two amounts shown?
"Current expenses" - money that usually actually leaves the family budget during the month: insurance, energy, maintenance, parking and roads. "Total Cost of Ownership" additionally takes into account the loss of value of the vehicle and the notional value of the invested capital. These two items are not separate monthly bills.
✳️ What do lines with an asterisk mean?
✳️ Notional cost of capital

Not a payment or a bank account. This is the price of having the money invested in the car and not sitting on deposit or otherwise not working. We calculate at the model 6.5% per annum - just for a fair comparison of options.

✳️ Loss of value

A car becomes cheaper over time. We are dividing this expected price reduction into months for comparison purposes only - you are not paying this amount every month, it will show up later when you sell.

How can I pay for the purchase of a car?

The conventional car loan, the large balance payment scheme at the end and the private leasing are explained separately below. These are different contracts - they cannot be compared only based on the small monthly payment.

1. Regular car loan

After the down payment, the remaining debt is gradually paid off in monthly payments. At the end of the term, there is usually no separate large debt left.

2. Large remaining payment

Part of the debt is deferred until the end of the contract. In Israel it is often called by the English word “balloon”. The monthly payment is lower because this portion is not yet repaid.

3. Private leasing

Use of a car under an agreement with a leasing company. The terms of return, redemption, mileage, insurance and service are determined by a specific contract.

The remaining payment is not a discount. For example, if 40% of the cost is left at the end, this amount will still have to be paid, refinanced, or covered by selling or exchanging the car. Interest may also accrue on this outstanding portion.

How is financial leasing different from operating leasing?
Financial leasing is primarily a method of financing: maintenance, insurance and running costs are often left to the client, and at the end there may be a buyout or refund. Private operating leasing may include maintenance, registration, insurance and roadside assistance, but the monthly payment is usually higher. The exact range of services is determined by the contract - there is no identical package for all companies.

What does "cost of capital" mean?
This is not a payment to the bank and not a mandatory monthly expense. This is the conditional price of the money invested in the car. For example, if ₪75,000 is spent on a car, these funds can no longer be kept on deposit, invested or used to reduce another loan. For comparison, the calculator uses a model 6.5% per annum. When buying on credit, instead of such a guideline, you need to look at the actual interest on the contract and not count the same expense twice.
Why is loss of value taken into account?
Over time, the car usually sells for less than the original price. The calculator distributes the estimated price reduction by month only to compare options. This is not a monthly payment: the loss becomes apparent when the car is subsequently sold.
What hasn't been taken into account yet?
Specific model, vehicle age, accident history, home or public charging, cost of installing a charging station, tires, unexpected major repairs, subsidies, taxes and individual insurance company discounts.
What is richion and what does eco-class have to do with it?
Annual permit to travel on the road (richayon, רישיון רכב) includes road tax to the state. Its size depends on the environmental class of the car (ramat zihum, רמת זיהום) - the older and “dirtier” the car’s emissions are, the higher the annual amount. For new economical models this is usually hundreds of shekels per year, for older, less environmentally friendly models - much more. The exact amount for a specific model and year of manufacture is established by the Ministry of Transport.
Why is insurance more expensive for a young driver?
Driving experience for calculating insurance in Israel is usually counted not from the moment of obtaining a license in another country, but from the moment of receiving an Israeli driver’s license. If you moved to Israel with many years of driving experience, for the insurance company you can still be considered a novice driver - and therefore fall into a more expensive risk category. This should be clarified with the insurance company in advance, and not after the policy has been calculated.

Important clarification

The calculation is intended as a preliminary comparison of scenarios and does not constitute a price offer, insurance quote, or recommendation to purchase a specific vehicle. Actual costs may vary significantly due to vehicle model and age, insurance history, fuel and electricity costs, loan terms, remaining balance or lease payment, actual mileage, repairs and future sales price. Before purchasing, check the current offers of the seller, bank, leasing and insurance company.
