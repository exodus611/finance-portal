What the law allows, what article establishes it and what needs to be done

Data updated August 4, 2026 from official sources

What is it based on?

The Arnona discount is maintained at three different levels. It is important not to confuse them: percentages are not in the law, but in by-laws, and the final figure is announced by your mayor’s office.

The most important thing: “up to 90%” and “90%” are not the same thing
מועצה רשאית לקבוע הנחה… הנחה שאינה עולה על 90 אחוזים
“The council has the right to establish a discount... a discount not exceeding 90 percent.”

The word “rashait” (רשאית) means “has the right”, not “obligation”. And “sheeina olá al” (שאינה עולה על) means “not exceeding.”

This means that the indicated percentage is an upper limit, and not the promised value. The final figure is announced by the mayor's office in its tsav arnona (צו ארנונה) for the current year.

That's why this page says "before" everywhere. To find out the exact figure for your city, ask the Arnona collection department or look up the Arnona collection on the city hall website.

A separate case is the elderly. Their discount is also based on the independent law Hok aezrahim avatikim (חוק האזרחים הוותיקים, התשנ״ט–1989), Article 9 (ג), where the rules are stricter and there is an income check.

If you are a repatriate

This is the simplest case of all. There is no income verification, no commission is collected, the decision does not depend on anyone’s discretion: either you meet the deadline or you don’t.

ole עולה 2(א)(6)(א) and 2(א)(6)(ב)

Discount up to 90 percent on the first 100 square meters of the apartment, for 12 months out of the first 24.

      At smaller discount rates—70, 50, and 30 percent—income thresholds are higher. For a family of 10 people, a fixed amount is added to the threshold of nine for each additional person.

      The amounts are revised on January 1 of each year based on changes in the minimum wage - their value is compared on May 20 of the two previous years. The updated table is published by the Director General of the Ministry of Internal Affairs.

      From 2026, average monthly income is calculated only based on the 12-month average. The ability to select a 3-month average has been removed.

      What to do step by step
        If you do not agree with the calculation

        You can challenge it, for example, if the invoice indicates an area larger than the real one or the payer is not the one who lives in the apartment. The deadlines are tight, so it’s important not to procrastinate.

        Filing an objection does not exempt you from paying the arnona. You must continue to pay while the proceedings are ongoing.
