Avtala (דמי אבטלה, unemployment benefits) - unemployment benefits in Israel

Short: "dmey avtala" (דמי אבטלה), unemployment benefits - This is unemployment benefit. You paid your premiums while you were working, and now this insurance comes back to you: for several months, the state supports you with part of your previous salary while you look for a new job.

Have you been fired? Calm down - everything can be solved

The first reaction is usually confusion: it is not clear where to go, what papers are needed and what you are entitled to. The good news is that the procedure is simple and straightforward. You need to go through three steps in order - and support from the state will come.

This page will take you through the entire process: what to ask your employer, where to register, what happens next, and how much you will be paid. There is no need to rush, but there is no need to delay either - the date of registration determines from what day payments will begin to be counted.

Step 1. What to collect from the employer

Start here - it's the easiest step and saves a lot of time later. While the connection with the employer is still fresh, documents are given quickly and without questions. After a month or two, getting through to the accounting department can be noticeably more difficult.

Everything you need to ask for is in the list below. The employer is required to provide most items by law, so this is not a favor, but a normal procedure.

    Reception with deadlines. A certificate of period of work must be issued within 14 days from the last working day. But if you ask in writing - the period is reduced to 7 days. Write by email or messenger to receive confirmation of shipment.

    What should a termination letter look like?

    This is the main document for the manual. In Hebrew - "mikhtav piturim" (מכתב פיטורים), mikhtav piturim. Usually a letter of resignation and a certificate of period of work - this is one document, you don’t need to ask for anything separately. The main thing is that it contains four things:

    1. Reason for termination in plain text: fired, quit on his own, sent to HALAT at the initiative of the employer, retired. This wording determines whether they will start paying immediately or through 90 days.
    2. Exact date finishing work.
    3. Start and end dates labor relations.
    4. Signature and seal employer.

    The employer is required by law to issue such a document - regardless of whether you were fired or you left yourself.

    About salary data: ask one phrase

    In Bituach Leumi, everyone used to carry a pack of payslips. Now it’s different: from October 2021 all employers are required to transmit data for each employee electronically on a monthly basis - this is called “form 100” (טופס 100). They don’t give you any form, it’s a machine transfer from the salary program directly to Bituach Leumi (National Insurance).

    Bituach Leumi puts it this way: “your employer must submit Form 100—you just need to make sure they do so.”. So call the accounting department and literally ask:

    “Have you submitted Form 100 to Bituach Leumi indicating the date and reason for the termination of my employment?”

    The answer is yes — you don’t need to attach pay slips, the data is already there.
    The answer is no - ask to fill out and sign the certificate BL/1514 about the period of work and salary. This is the same data, but manually.

    Don't be confused: there is also a printable Annual Form 100 - Summary of Salary and Insurance Contributions that you can request going back 7 years. It is needed to reconcile contributions, not for benefits.

    Step 2. Where to go: three departments and one form

    The main confusion is at the very beginning: people think that “registering an avtala” is one action in one place. In fact, they are involved in the process three independent organizations, and they work sequentially, transferring data to each other.

    Remember the division. Employment service (שירות התעסוקה) keeps track of the fact that you are looking for work and notes your attendance - but doesn't pay money. Bituach Leumi (ביטוח לאומי) pays money - but does not monitor attendance himself, and receives a report from the Service once a month. If you missed a mark in the Service, Bituach Leumi simply won’t charge you for these days, and there will be nowhere to complain.
    Good news: from 2023 there is a unified form. Previously, it was necessary to register separately with the Employment Service and separately submit a claim to Bituach Leumi (National Insurance), duplicating documents. Now one online form on the MyGov portal through the national identification system is going away to both departments at once.

    The date of filing is considered both the date of registration with the Service and the date of commencement of benefit eligibility. The only condition is to appear at the bureau within 15 days after submission.

    Who serves what?

    • You: combined form, attendance at grades, sending missing documents through your personal account.
    • The employer directly, without your participation: salary data in Bituach Leumi, notification to the pension fund about the release of savings (within 15 days).

    What happens after submission

    The employment service usually calls itself

    After submitting the form, you will receive registration confirmation via SMS or email. Then, as a rule, within a few days the Service worker calls — opens a case, schedules appearances, and sometimes conducts an initial employment interview.

    If no call for a week - don’t wait, go to the office in person. Without an open case and scheduled appearances, days are not counted, and therefore no money is awarded. Check the opening hours of your branch in advance and keep in mind that there may be a queue there.

    One deadline to keep an eye on. After online registration, you must appear at the office within 15 days. If you don’t make it in time, you will have to register again, and along with it, the start date for payments will shift. Set yourself a reminder - this is enough.

    Personal account: all correspondence goes there

    A thing that few people understand right away: There is almost never a need to go to the Bituach Leumi branch. Almost all communication takes place through your personal account on the website. Get it as soon as you submit your request.

    • If for consideration something is missing — the request will appear in your personal account and will be duplicated SMS to phone. Documents are sent there, online.
    • Viden claim status: accepted, in progress, approved, rejected.
    • Visible after approval amounts, payment dates and remaining days.
    • Stored entire correspondence history - useful in case of dispute or appeal.

    Check your account at least once a week: the document request has a deadline, so it is better to respond on time.

    The first five days are not paid - but they are not lost

    A rule that surprises almost everyone. In law No money is paid for the first 5 days of unemployment. In Hebrew they are called "yemei amtana" (ימי המתנה), yemey amtana - days of waiting. The countdown starts from the date of the first appearance at the Employment Service.

    But here’s a detail that is almost never written about. These 5 days are not deducted from your quota: if 138 days are allotted, there will be 138 of them left. Waiting days only delay the start of payments.

    Moreover, the law provides that these days paid later at the end of the period - provided that you continued to show up for assessments and a job was never found for you. Money is not lost, but saved.

    Another nuance: retention is applied once every 4 months of continuous attendance. If you went back to work, then were left without it again and a new period began, you may be detained again for five days.

    During times of war, the state sometimes abolishes waiting days by temporary regulation - this was the case under HALAT in 2020, 2023 and 2026. If the dismissal is related to a military situation, check the current exemptions.

    When the money comes

      For the first month, the payment arrives on a variable date - it depends on when the claim was submitted and how quickly the department processed it. From the second month, payments go out approximately on the 12th - for the days of attendance of the previous month.

      The request must be submitted within 12 months from the date of first appearance at the bureau. If you are late, you will not be paid for the past period.

      Why the amount is exactly this: how the calculation works

      Unemployment benefits in Hebrew are called "dmey avtala" (דמי אבטלה), pronounced "unemployment benefits". The word "avtala" (אבטלה) and means “unemployment”, so simply “avtala” has taken root in Russian speech in Israel.

      The logic is this: while you were working, contributions to Bituach Leumi were withheld from your salary every month (ביטוח לאומי), "bituach leumi" — National Insurance Office. This is insurance in case of job loss. When the job ends against your will, the insurance begins to pay out.

      Why is the amount less than expected?

      There are two mechanisms here, and both work against high wages.

      The first is a step scale. The benefit is not calculated as “so many percent of the salary.” Your average daily earnings are cut into parts, and each part is calculated at its own rate - the higher the part, the lower the percentage. The first shekels are reimbursed generously, the last shekels sparingly.

      The second is the ceiling. No matter how much you earn, more 550.76 per day won't pay. And starting from 125-day payout ceiling drops to 367.17. For those who received 30–40 thousand, this is the main unpleasant surprise: the benefit turns out to be about a third of their previous income, and after five months it is even less.

            Frequently asked questions
            How much do unemployment pay in Israel?

            Depends on salary, age and number of dependents. The calculation is stepwise: parts of earnings are calculated at different percentages - from 80% for the first part to 30% for the top (for those under 28 years old, the rates are lower). Top ceiling: 550.76 first on the day 125 days, then 367.17. The calculator above will show the exact amount for your case.

            How many days of autala are required?

            From 50 to 175 days depending on age and dependents. From 45 years old - maximum 175 days. Up to 25 years without dependents - only 50. In the first year after the army - up to 70 days. The full table is above on the page.

            What is tkufat akhshara and how to count it?

            This is the required experience: 12 months of employment in the last 18. The months do not have to be consecutive and can be with different employers. Key point: Even one paid work day in a calendar month counts as a full month.

            Will they give me an avtala if I quit my job?

            Yes, but with a delay 90 days. If the reason for leaving is recognized as valid - a significant deterioration in conditions, health, moving more than 60 km, childbirth - they pay from the first day, but documents are needed.

            Why do people under 28 pay less?

            This is how the law works: for those who have not yet turned 28, the percentage of compensation at each level is lower. The legislator’s logic is that it is easier for young people to find a new job. In practice, this means a noticeably smaller amount for the same salary.

            Is it possible to earn extra money while receiving avtala?

            Part-time work reduces benefits, and going back to work for your previous employer, even for a few days, deprives you of payments for the entire month. If the bureau offers a job with a pay lower than the allowance, you can receive additional payment of the difference - but subject to working at least 25 days part-time or more.

            What to do if refused?

            The decision can be appealed. First, you should understand the reason for the refusal from the notice: most often it is a lack of experience or a dispute about the reason for dismissal. Many refusals are lifted after the employer submits the missing documents.

            Why didn't they pay for the first days?

            These are “waiting days” (ימי המתנה): no money is paid for the first 5 days of unemployment. But they are not deducted from your quota of days, and according to the law they are paid later, at the end of the period - if you continued to show up for your assessments and a job was not found for you. The deduction is applied once every 4 months of continuous attendance.

            Do I need to go to the Bituach Leumi (National Insurance) branch?

            Almost never. All correspondence goes through the personal account on the website: requests for missing documents appear there, they are sent there, and the status of the request and the amount are visible there. Each request additionally receives an SMS. Create an account immediately after submitting your request.

            Will they call me from the Employment Service or should I go myself?

            Usually they call themselves within a few days after registration: they open a case and set a schedule of appearances. If you haven’t received a call in a week, go to the office in person, otherwise the days will not be counted. In any case, you must appear within 15 days after online submission.

            When does the money arrive?

            For the first month - on a variable date, depending on the processing speed. From the second month, payments are made approximately on the 12th for the days of attendance of the previous month.

            Is Avtala taxable?

            Yes. Income tax is withheld at regular levels, contribution to Bituach Leumi (National Insurance) (48 per month) and health insurance. If you have a low income, you can apply for an income tax exemption.

            I already received avala recently - will they give me the same amount the second time?

            If you are under 40 years old and you apply again within 4 years from the beginning of the last payment period, the second time they will not give you the full amount, but 80% of allotted days and amounts. If there have already been two payments during these 4 years, a new one will not be given at all until the end of this period. After 40 years, this restriction does not apply.

            I am in the reservist service (miluim) - does this prevent me from receiving avtala?

            No. If you quit for a good reason and were sent to reservist training during the 90-day waiting period, the time of service does not shift or cancel the right to payments, it is simply counted separately and does not count towards the days of aval.

            Is it possible to study or undergo retraining while receiving an aval?

            Yes, provided that this vocational training is approved by the employment service. Usually, a tuition stipend is deducted from payments (about 70% of the total amount), but for a number of in-demand professions (electrics, welding, mechanics and similar) - the additional payment is without deduction, that is, the person receives 100%, not 70%.

            What else are you entitled to besides benefits?

            Avtala is only part of the money upon dismissal. The rest goes through other channels, and this is often forgotten:

But you should pick it up at the same time, along with the rest of the documents: then it’s more difficult to contact your former employer. Details on compensation and taxes are a separate topic.
