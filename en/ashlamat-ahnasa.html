Amounts of Bituach Leumi (National Insurance) · 2026 Ashlamat akhnas and “social bonus” are same thing: Bituach Leumi contributes to the old-age (or survivors) pension up to a certain income ceiling. Enter your numbers - you will see at what income the allowance is due and how many shekels will be added.

This is an assessment and not a decision of Bituach Leumi. The National Insurance Institute calculates eligibility according to its data: length of insurance, family composition, property, stay abroad, “conditional income” from capital and real estate. Our calculation shows the order of the numbers and tells you what to check. Amounts - 2026, source: btl.gov.il and pravoton “Kol zkhut”.

How to apply

  1. Check in your personal account for btl.gov.il or in the letter of pension assignment, what amount was accrued to you and how much length of service was taken into account.
  2. Fill out the form «בקשה לתוספת השלמת הכנסה» (application for social supplement) - it can be submitted immediately with the pension application or separately later if income has dropped.
  3. Attach: account statements for 12 months, certificates from the bank on deposits and from the pension fund, documents on income and property (including a car and real estate).
  4. Submit to the BC branch, by mail/fax or through your personal account. The bonus is usually assigned from the month of submission.
  5. If the right existed earlier, and you filed later - You can request retroactive payment, usually up to 12 months. Attach bank statements for this period.
  6. The supplement is tied to residence in Israel—departures abroad must be reported.

Do not confuse: ashlamat akhnasa, avtakhat akhnasa and avtala

The short answer is that they are different payments. Both grew out of the same law (חוק הבטחת הכנסה, תש״מ-1980), both check income and savings - but pay them in different situations. One law gave two mechanisms: complete the benefit is called הבטחת הכנסה, and partial — השלמת הכנסה.

Frequently asked questions

Ashlamat akhnasa and avtakhat akhnasa - what is the difference?

Different. Ashlamat akhnas (השלמת הכנסה) — partial benefit: additional payment to a minimum to an already assigned old-age or survivors' pension. Avtakhat akhnasa (הבטחת הכנסה) — complete benefits for those who have neither income nor the right to other benefits; the residency condition is stricter - at least 24 months of continuous residence in Israel. The amounts are also different: in 2026, the supplement brings the pension of a single person to 4,375–4,460 ₪, and avtakhat akhnas is 2,076 ₪ (25–54 years old) or 2,596 ₪ (55 years old is the retirement age). Detailed table is in the section above.

I have an apartment - does this “throw me out” of my right?

The apartment in which you live, as a rule, does not deprive you of your right to a supplement. Real estate beyond this is taken into account - according to cost scales (2026: from ~293,000 ₪ for one, its own steps for a couple), sometimes taking into account the fact that a person can sell it. This is the case where a check in the BC is needed, and not a calculation using a calculator.

Why does a neighbor with the same pension get a larger bonus?

The ceiling depends on family composition and age, and also takes into account length of service (increase for length of service - 2% for each year over 10, maximum 50% of the base) and deferment of receiving a pension (+5% for each year). Sometimes the difference is simply because the person applied for the supplement separately.

We receive child benefit - is this considered income?

Bituach Leumi benefits, which are considered “basic” (pension, child benefit), are included in the supplement mechanism according to special rules; more often, income from a pension fund or work “removes” the right. If in doubt, attach all the certificates to the application: BL will recalculate it itself, and you can appeal if the calculation seems incorrect.
