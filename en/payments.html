  • Salaried employees aged 67 years and older, residents of Israel, who were fired or sent on unpaid leave (robe) from 02/28/2026 for at least 10 days. If the vacation started on February 28 or March 1, 5 days are enough.

Compensation of earnings for those who are over retirement age and, as a rule, do not receive unemployment benefits, but lost their jobs due to the war.

Early payment is not extra money

Before the holidays, Bituach Leumi pays regular benefits earlier than the usual date. The amount is the same, only the day changes. Example: In September 2026, unemployment and income security came on September 8 (Rosh Hashanah), and all long-term benefits came on September 22 instead of the 28th (Sukkot). Bituach Leumi announced this in advance, on August 23.

Debt for the past comes in one payment

For example, if you were assigned a disability benefit, Bituach Leumi (National Insurance) transfers in one payment the entire amount for the period from the moment you became eligible, and then pays monthly on the 28th (official response from Bituach Leumi).

How to find out about new payments

The list is updated automatically: twice a day the site checks the data with official announcements of Bituach Leumi (National Insurance). The advertisements themselves are published in the section "News" on the Bituach Leumi website and in your mybtl personal account. Bituach Leumi phone number: *6050, for the elderly - *9696.

How dates work

Transfer rules and where to check the official schedule

  1. Numbers in groups are stableUnemployment and income security around the 12th, self-employed and employer contributions around the 15th, child benefit around the 20th, long-term benefits around the 28th. The specific date for each month is determined by Bituach Leumi and published in advance.
  2. Friday, Saturday and holiday - payment earlierIf the date falls on a Friday, Saturday or an Israeli holiday, Bituach Leumi usually pays earlier - on the previous working day, and sometimes several days before major holidays (in September 2026, long-term benefits arrived on the 22nd instead of the 28th). Therefore, the number may differ from month to month.
  3. Official scheduleBituach Leumi publishes it monthly on the website: Benefit Payment Dates page. The table on this page is taken from there and updated automatically.
  4. Where to check your paymentMybtl personal account - you can see there that the payment is “processing” and when it will be completed. Bituach Leumi (National Insurance) phone number: *9696 or 04-8812345.

Payments to new repatriates

Saal klita and rental assistance - according to the date of your aliyah, and not according to the general calendar

✈️ Saal klita (absorption basket)

Paid by the Ministry of Aliyah and Integration. Part - in cash at the airport upon entry, the rest - 6 monthly payments on account. Amounts 2026: single ≈20,491 ₪, couple ≈39,065 ₪, family with 2 children ≈61,000 ₪ (for the first six months). The exact payment date depends on the date of your repatriation and is not the same for everyone.

🏠 Apartment after saal klit

Ministry of Construction and Housing (a separate agency from Bituach Leumi). Rent assistance continues after the end of Saal Klit - until the end of the 5th year of repatriation (6th for single parents). When payments begin depends on the exact date of your repatriation.

Questions and answers

Briefly about what is asked most often

When is the Bituach Leumi payment this month?

Four groups: unemployment benefits and income guarantee - around the 12th; contributions of self-employed and employers - on the 15th; child benefit - 20th; long-term benefits (old age, disability, care - siyud and others) - 28th. If the date falls on a Friday, Saturday or holiday, the payment is usually postponed to an earlier day. The exact dates are in the table above.

Why has the date moved relative to last month?

If the day of the month falls on a Friday, Saturday or holiday, Bituach Leumi usually pays earlier - on the previous working day, and sometimes several days before major holidays. This is normal practice and not a bug.

The money did not arrive on the date - what should I do?

First, go to your mybtl account: the payment may be listed as “processing”. Then call *9696 or 04-8812345 or visit the branch. If they arrive late, ask them to explain the reason.

What do they pay on the day of long-term benefits?

Old age benefits (קצבת אזרח ותיק), survivor benefits (קצבת שארים), disability benefits (קצבת נכות), for a disabled child, special services for severely disabled people, for mobility, for those injured at work, alimony, rehabilitation, care (סיעוד) and prisoners of Zion. The list is in section 2.

What extraordinary payments does Bituach Leumi (National Insurance) pay?

Outside of the monthly schedule, Bituach Leumi (National Insurance) pays annual heating benefits (in October to the elderly, in November to victims of terrorist attacks), a school grant for single-parent families and large families (in August) and special payments decided by the government and the Knesset - for example, payments to employees 67+ who lost their jobs due to the war. To whom, when, how much and for what - in the “Extraordinary and one-time payments” section on this page (2026 amounts: heating for the elderly - 664 ₪ per family, school grant - 1,204 ₪ per child).

How can I find out about a new extraordinary payment?

Bituach Leumi publishes announcements in the “News” section on btl.gov.il and in the mybtl personal account. We are updating the list on this page. If you think that you are entitled to a payment, but there is no money, call: *6050 (for seniors - *9696).
